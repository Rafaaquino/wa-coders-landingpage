Frequently asked questions

Before you get in touch, you may want to know:

How does the WA Coders nearshore model work?

We have offices in Miami and São Paulo and work in your company’s time zone, whether in the U.S., Europe or Brazil. Collaboration happens in real time, with fluent English communication and documentation at every stage.

What services does WA Coders offer?

Custom software development, web development, mobile apps (iOS and Android), dedicated teams, UX/UI design, integrations and legacy modernization, and SAP Business One and CRM C4C customization.

Do you implement AI, chatbots and RAG?

Yes. We design and integrate artificial intelligence solutions, such as chatbots, virtual assistants and RAG (retrieval-augmented generation), connected to your company’s documents, databases and systems, such as CRM and ERP.

Which technologies do you work with?

Angular, React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Java, .NET, React Native, Flutter, Swift, Android, MongoDB, MySQL, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, WordPress and Figma, plus SAP Business One/C4C, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and AI models such as Claude (Anthropic) and GPT (OpenAI).

How does hiring a dedicated team work?

Engineers join your team on a flexible monthly contract, with no recruiting process and no long-term commitment. Capacity scales up or down as your roadmap changes.

How do I request a proposal, and how fast do you reply?

Fill out the contact form. We reply within 24 hours, learn about your business, discuss team structure, timeline and budget and, within days, finalize the project specifications.

Do you customize SAP Business One and CRM C4C?

Yes. We build a customization layer on top of your SAP Business One and CRM C4C data, starting from Opportunity, our production-proven platform, and tailored to your approval workflows, rules and branding.
