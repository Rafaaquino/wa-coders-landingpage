What we do

Software Development Services

From your first idea to a live product in your customers' hands — we design, build, and maintain the software your business runs on. Every engagement is staffed with senior engineers who write production-ready code from day one, not junior developers learning on your budget.

Custom Software Development

Full-stack applications built around your business logic — from internal tools to customer-facing platforms. We handle architecture, database design, APIs, and the front-end interfaces your team and customers interact with every day, all built to scale as you grow.

Web Development

Fast, responsive websites and web applications built with modern frameworks like Angular and React. We optimize every page for conversion, page speed, and search visibility, so your site turns visitors into leads instead of just looking good.

Mobile App Development

Native and cross-platform iOS and Android apps, built for performance and a seamless user experience. From the first wireframe to App Store and Google Play submission, we handle the full mobile development lifecycle.

Dedicated Development Teams

Senior engineers embedded directly in your team on a flexible monthly contract — no recruiting, onboarding delays, or long-term commitment. Scale your engineering capacity up or down as your roadmap changes, without the overhead of local hiring.

UX/UI Design

User research, wireframing, and interface design that turn complex products into experiences your customers actually enjoy using. We design in Figma, validate with real users, and hand off production-ready components your developers can implement directly.

Integrations & Modernization

Connect your CRM, payment gateway, ERP, or any third-party system to the software you already have. We also migrate legacy platforms to modern stacks without disrupting the operations that depend on them.

Frequently asked questions

Before you talk to us, you probably want to know:

How does the WA Coders nearshore model work?

We are a senior team with offices in Miami and São Paulo. We work in your company’s time zone in the U.S., Europe or Brazil, with real-time collaboration, fluent English communication and documented handoffs at every stage.

What services does WA Coders offer?

Custom software development, web development, mobile apps (iOS and Android), dedicated development teams, UX/UI design, integrations and legacy modernization, and SAP Business One and CRM C4C customization.

Which technologies do you work with?

Angular, React, Node.js, Java, .NET, React Native, Flutter, Swift, Android, MongoDB, MySQL, Docker, AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and WordPress.

Senior engineers join your team directly on a flexible monthly contract — no recruiting process and no long-term commitment. You scale capacity up or down as your roadmap changes.
