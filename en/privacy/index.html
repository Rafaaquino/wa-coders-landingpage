02What data we collect

  • Data you provide in forms: name, email, subject and message. In the proposal request forms on the SAP/CRM and Dynamics/CRM pages, also company, job title, phone number, whether you already use SAP Business One or Dynamics 365, and your topic of interest.
  • Technical data sent with the form: the site language and the page it was sent from.
  • Browsing data, collected only if you accept analytics cookies: IP address, device type, browser, pages visited, time on page, clicks, scrolling and traffic source.
  • Preferences stored in your browser: your chosen language and your cookie decision.
  • When you click the WhatsApp button, you are taken to the app and the conversation follows WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

The site also uses your browser’s local storage (which is not cookies) to remember your chosen language and your cookie decision.

05Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the site, to the extent necessary:

  • Resend: delivery of the emails generated by the forms.
  • Vercel: hosting of the API that receives the forms.
  • GitHub: hosting of the website.
  • Google (Analytics and Tag Manager) and Hotjar: usage analytics, only with your consent.

We may also share data with authorities when required by law or court order.

06International transfers

The providers above may process data outside Brazil, including in the United States and the European Union. In these cases we seek to ensure an adequate level of protection through standard contractual clauses, adequacy decisions or other mechanisms provided by the LGPD and the GDPR, as applicable.

07How long we keep data

Messages and proposal requests: for as long as needed to handle your request, follow up on the business relationship and comply with legal obligations. After that, data is deleted or anonymized.

Analytics data: for the retention period configured in each tool.

08Your rights

You may, at any time, request:

  • confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed in non-compliance;
  • data portability;
  • deletion of data processed on the basis of your consent;
  • information about who we share your data with;
  • withdrawal of consent, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • objection to processing and, in the European Union, restriction of processing.

To exercise these rights, write to contact@wacoders.com. We respond within the legal deadlines. You may also lodge a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or with the data protection authority of your country in the European Union.

09Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect data, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to the messages we receive. No system is completely immune to incidents and, if one occurs that may affect you, we will notify you as required by law.

10Minors

The site is not directed at people under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.
