Frequently asked questions

Before you get in touch, you may want to know:

Do I need to switch CRMs to use this customization?

No. The solution connects to the Dynamics your company already uses: it organizes and presents the data and does not replace the CRM.

Which data can be visualized?

Client, account, opportunity and project data from Dynamics, combined with financial data such as revenue, costs and margin. We define with you which indicators make sense for each area.

How does the project start?

We start by understanding which decisions your management needs to make and which data lives in Dynamics and in the financial systems. Then we design the views, validate them with you and evolve in stages.

Does WA Coders do the customization for my company?

Yes. Each dashboard is designed and built to fit your operation, with your branding and your business rules.
